# Third-party assets

## Apple

`public/models/iphone-duo.usdz` and `public/models/iphone-duo.glb` derive from Apple's [iPhone Duo product page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/): a prepared landscape pose and a converted model with split folding geometry, adjusted materials, and replaceable screen surfaces, including textures from Apple's model.

The original model, textures, product design, and Apple trademarks remain the property of Apple. These assets are excluded from this repository's MIT license. ## Folding component

`src/iphone-duo/` and the model preparation scripts come from [jal-co/iphone-duo](https://github.com/jal-co/iphone-duo) under the MIT license (see `LICENSE`).
